## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, contemporary South Indian heritage tailored for discerning travelers seeking quiet luxury, authenticity, and restorative stays. The brand evokes the calm courtyard architecture of Chettinad and Kerala illams, filtered through modern editorial minimalism: spacious, tactile, warm, and sunlit.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Grounded Opulence:** Replaces loud luxury with tactile simplicity—handwoven textures, terracotta tones, teakwood calm, and warm brass accents.
- **Warm Hospitality (Atithi Devo Bhava):** Intuitive navigation, welcoming negative space, generous touch targets, and considerate typography.
- **Rooted Modernity:** Heritage motifs (delicate kolam geometry, brass inlays, rhythmic verandah pillars) reduced to clean, structural design lines.

### Design Movement
**Heritage Warm Minimal:** Combines high-end hospitality editorial layouts with warm ivory backgrounds, soft-curved organic surfaces, deep madder-maroon anchors, and hairline brass accents. Interfaces avoid sterile grayness, adopting an ambient biscuit-and-terracotta glow throughout.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from native South Indian architectural elements: dried madder root (Manjistha), Athangudi tile ochres, weathered brass hardware, and lime-plastered walls.

### Palette Breakdown
- **Primary (`#801323` - Terracotta Crimson / Deep Maroon):** Used for primary CTAs, active states, key brand emblems, and emphasized prices. Evokes sacred vermilion and sun-baked clay.
- **Secondary (`#C89D56` - Temple Brass / Warm Gold):** Reserved for delicate ornamental highlights, star ratings, premium badges, and subtle rule dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#E27977` - Blush Salmon):** Soft accent for gentle notifications, category tags, hover accents, and festive ribbons.
- **Neutral Core (`#231F20` - Espresso Charcoal):** High-contrast text color offering warm readability superior to pure pitch black.
- **Secondary Text (`#6E645F` - Muted Brown-Stone):** For captions, metadata, dates, and secondary interface guidance.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Warm Ivory / Calcified Chalk):** Primary canvas background that softens glare and sets an ambient mood.
- **Surface Raised (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for interactive cards, booking modules, floating modals, and image backplates.
- **Surface Sunlit Accent (`#FEF0D2` - Warm Biscuit / Sand):** Subtle container wash for hero banners, search panels, and testimonial sections.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances dramatic editorial luxury with crystal-clear utility. 

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the poise of a boutique heritage retreat. Used for property titles, hero taglines, room categories, and editorial narratives. Italicized cuts may be sparingly applied to single evocative words (e.g., *serene* verandah stays).
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its generous counters, modern geometric skeleton, and soft terminal curves harmonize cleanly with high-contrast serif headlines without causing reading fatigue.
- **Label Editorial:** Used for small caps overlines, location tags (e.g., `CHETTINAD, TAMIL NADU`), and curated collection pre-headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emulates the architectural courtyard (*Muttaram* / *Thotti*), utilizing a generous, unhurried 12-column grid anchored by expansive margins.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, dynamic outer margins scaling up to a centered `1280px` max-width container.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px page margins.

### Rhythmic Layout Rules
- **Verandah Spacing:** Editorial content alternates between asymmetric photo/copy spreads and centered, columned showcases.
- **Vertical Breathing Room:** Major sections must maintain `space-3xl` (desktop) and `space-2xl` (mobile) separations to preserve an airy retreat ambiance.
- **Sticky Booking Float:** On mobile screens, reservations compress into a persistent, rounded bottom floating rail that leaves content unobscured.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain the tactile warmth of handcrafted hospitality, elevation avoids cold, artificial gray shadows. All depth uses warm ambient lighting tinted with umber and terracotta.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Flat Ground (`0dp`):** `#FDFBF7` baseline canvas.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Cards):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with an ambient shadow:  
  `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(62, 56, 53, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(62, 56, 53, 0.03)`  
  Paired with a hairline warm biscuit border (`1px solid #F5E6CC`).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Calendar Pickers, Hover States):**  
  `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(128, 19, 35, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(62, 56, 53, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Widget, Modals):**  
  `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(128, 19, 35, 0.04)`.

### Decorative Separation
Rather than standard gray hairline dividers, demarcate sections with an ultra-fine brass line (`#C89D56`, 20% opacity) or a centered mini-mandala ornamental glyph (`16px`) in tertiary blush or brass.

## Shapes

The interface embraces generous, organic architectural curves reminiscent of traditional arched hallways, brass urns, and sculpted teak woodwork.

### Corner Radii
- **Standard UI Elements (`rounded-lg` / `16px`):** Inputs, interactive chips, amenity tags, tooltips.
- **Content Cards (`rounded-2xl` / `24px`):** Room cards, host bio panels, testimonial blocks.
- **Large Feature Panels & Modals (`rounded-3xl` / `32px`):** Floating booking bar, hero image frames, date picker dialogs.
- **Arch Frame Variant (`rounded-t-full rounded-b-2xl`):** An architectural arch silhouette used exclusively for curated feature photography and room showcases.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Terracotta Crimson (`#801323`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-xl` (16px), subtle inner brass glow on hover. Generous padding (`16px 28px`), typography set to `label-lg`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a crisp `1.5px` border in `#801323` or muted brass `#C89D56`, `#231F20` text, transitioning to `#FEF0D2` fill on hover.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Understated typography with a slow-expanding underline styled in `#C89D56`.

### Cards (Homestay / Room Showcase)
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-2xl` (24px) corners and hairline `#F5E6CC` border.
- Images feature a 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio with smooth inner radius conform.
- Badge indicators (e.g., "Heritage Villa", "Chettinad Suite") float top-left with a warm biscuit pill style (`#FEF0D2`, text `#801323`, `rounded-full`).
- Price displays use `Playfair Display` for numbers with a muted brown label for `/ night`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Deselected:** `#FDFBF7` surface, border `1px solid #F5E6CC`, text `#6E645F`.
- **Selected:** Filled `#801323` with `#FFFFFF` text, or filled `#FEF0D2` with `#801323` text and a deep terracotta check icon.
- Shape is pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `8px 16px` padding.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Homestay Search Bar:** Horizontal multi-part pill module (Location, Dates, Guests) resting on `#FFFFFF` with Level 2 elevation and `rounded-3xl` boundary.
- **Text Inputs:** Soft background `#FDFBF7` inset into white containers, `1px solid #F5E6CC` border, focusing to a crisp `#801323` border with `0 0 0 3px rgba(128, 19, 35, 0.1)`. Placeholder text in `#6E645F` (40% opacity).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Radio Buttons:** Outer circle `#C89D56`, active inner dot `#801323` on an ivory plate.
- **Checkboxes:** `rounded-md` (6px), active fill in `#801323` with a warm white SVG checkmark.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Amenity Badges:** Fine-line outline icons (e.g., traditional filter coffee, courtyard pool, brass Ayurvedic bath) encased in `#FEF0D2` circles.
- **Host Story Card:** Dual-panel component pairing an arch-cut portrait with warm editorial serif quotes and an authenticated badge.
- **Date Range Picker:** Custom calendar featuring terracotta dots for selected dates and a soft blush salmon (`#F4A698` at 25% opacity) highlight bar connecting the itinerary span.
